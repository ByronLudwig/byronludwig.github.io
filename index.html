Dot Art: Match the Picture

Copy the picture dot by dot on a magnetic pegboard.

Free · All ages · PEGI 3 · Android

A picture sits on the card above the board. Copy it, one coloured dot at a time, until the board matches it exactly. Tap a colour to load it, or drag a dot straight out of the tray — either way it snaps into the nearest hole and stays put. No timers, no streaks, no pressure: it is made for anyone who finds that sort of thing quietly satisfying, grown-ups included.

  • 🖼️155 pictures, 11 themesSky & garden, food, pets, wild animals, under the sea, faces and more. Every theme open from the start.
  • 📐Boards to grow intoEasy, medium and hard, each bigger than the last.
  • 💡Nudges, not naggingA hint points at the next missing dot when someone gets stuck.
  • ✏️Draw your ownFree-play boards up to 12×12, with mirror mode, save and share.
  • ⭐Go for the gold starSolve it with no dots wasted.
  • 🌙Plays day or nightDark mode for the evening wind-down.

Kids Pretend Shop

Run your own pretend cafe, toy store and sports store.

Free to start · Made for ages 3+ · PEGI 3 · Android

Kids love playing shop. Pretend Shop turns the device into their shop: take orders, fill the basket, watch the total add up, and "pay" at the till — big friendly buttons, fun sounds and a satisfying buzz on every tap.

  • 🏪Three shops to runThe Cafe, the Toy Store and the Sports Store, each with its own aisles. More on the way.
  • 💷Real little sumsEvery item has a price and the total grows as the basket fills.
  • 📲Tap to payHold two devices together for a pretend contactless payment.
  • 🥜What's in my food?Filter the menu by gluten, dairy, eggs or nuts. A gentle way in to talking about allergies.
  • 🦄Make it their shopThey pick the name, the logo and the background.
  • 🔒Made for little shopkeepersOne-off ad-free unlock, no subscriptions, purchases behind a parent gate.

About LudwigTech

LudwigTech is one person: Byron, a software engineer who started building apps for his own kids and kept going — sometimes for them, sometimes for himself. Every picture, price and sound effect has been play-tested by a fairly demanding focus group at the kitchen table.

There is no team, no investors and no growth targets — just apps I am happy to hand my own children, and one I play myself. If you download one, that genuinely helps: it is what keeps the next one getting built.

Want to help? Installing an app, leaving a review on Google Play, or passing it on to someone who would enjoy it all make a real difference.
