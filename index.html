Dot Art: Picture Puzzle

Copy the picture dot by dot on a magnetic pegboard.

Free to start · All ages · PEGI 3 · Android

A picture sits on the card above the board. Copy it, one coloured dot at a time, until the board matches it exactly. Tap a colour to load it, or drag a dot straight out of the tray — either way it snaps into the nearest hole. No timers, no lives, no pressure: it is made for anyone who finds that sort of thing quietly satisfying, grown-ups included.

  • 🖼️Lots of pictures, plenty of themesSky & garden, food, pets, wild animals, under the sea, faces and more. The first few themes are free; unlock the rest one at a time or all at once, with a one-off purchase.
  • 📅A new picture every dayA hand-drawn picture of the day you won't find on the shelf. Solve it to grow your streak and earn a hint, with two on every third day in a row. One missed day needn't end the run.
  • 📐Boards to grow intoEasy, medium and hard, each bigger than the last.
  • 💡Nudges, not naggingA hint points at the next missing dot when someone gets stuck.
  • ✏️Draw your ownFree-play boards up to 12×12, with mirror mode and save. Send a drawing as a picture, or as a challenge a friend can copy in the app.
  • ⭐Go for the gold starSolve it with no dots wasted. Dark mode, too, for the evening wind-down.

Kids Pretend Shop

Run your own pretend cafe, toy store, sports store and sweet shop.

Free to start · Made for ages 3+ · PEGI 3 · Android

Kids love playing shop. Pretend Shop turns the device into their shop: serve customers who say what they want, fill the tray, bag or sweet jar, watch the total add up, and "pay" at the till or by holding two devices together — big friendly buttons, every word read aloud and a satisfying buzz on every tap.

  • 🏪Shops to runThe Cafe, the Toy Store, the Sports Store and the Sweet Shop, and more on the way. They pick the name, the logo and the background.
  • 🙋Customers come to shopTap Play and a customer says their order out loud. Missed something? They say what they still want. No timers, no scores.
  • 🔊Hear every wordTap the speaker beside an item, shelf or total to hear it, so children who can't read yet can play on their own. Works offline.
  • 💷Little or BigLittle (3–4) is a picture-only shop. Big (5+) adds names, prices and a total that grows as the order fills.
  • 🥜What's in my food?Filter the menu by gluten, dairy, eggs or nuts. A gentle way in to talking about allergies.
  • 🔒Made for little shopkeepersNo ads, no subscriptions. One purchase unlocks every paid shop, behind a parent gate.

About LudwigTech

LudwigTech is one person: Byron, a software engineer who started building apps for his own kids and kept going — sometimes for them, sometimes for himself. Every picture, price and sound effect has been play-tested by a fairly demanding focus group at the kitchen table.

There is no team, no investors and no growth targets — just apps I am happy to hand my own children, and one I play myself. If you download one, that genuinely helps: it is what keeps the next one getting built.

Want to help? Installing an app, leaving a review on Google Play, or passing it on to someone who would enjoy it all make a real difference.
